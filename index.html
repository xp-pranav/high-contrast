01 — The short version

High Contrast is a corner espresso bar for people who read the origin line on the bag. We roast light, pull short, and keep the labels honest.

What started as a two-group pop-up in an empty butcher shop became a permanent room on Laneway Walk. Every bean is roasted twice a week on our 15-kilo drum, and every cup is dialled by hand first thing each morning. No syrups, no secret menu — just coffee with a clearly printed receipt.

04 — House rules

Three things we won't compromise on.

01

Roast light, label honestly

Single origin, farm and process printed on every bag and ticket. If it's not traceable, we don't pull it.

02

No secret menus

What's on the board is what we've got. When it's gone, it's gone — and we'll tell you before you order.

03

The room runs on regulars

We learn names, not just orders. Walk-ins only, because we like the queue better that way.

— the fine print, out in the open.
